### 1.1 Did it end up what the spec said it should be?

Yes, to a degree that is unusual for a first build. Screens 1–8 of `design-scope.md` and journeys J1–J14 are all implemented, with the states the spec enumerates (slow import, failed analysis, offline, conflict, future time, under-18 stop, no-plan Today). The rubric in `lib/rubric` matches product-spec § 8 exactly: 50/30/20 per meal, 90/10 day, number only after two scored meals, bands at 85/60, skipped slots in coverage but not score, "Other" meals in nutrition only, no stored score.

### 1.2 Deviations that change what the user experiences

1. **Invented noon.** Unchecking "I don't remember the time" prefills `12:00` ([MealComposer.tsx:316](src/components/product/MealComposer.tsx#L316)). Product-spec § 7 forbids inventing a timestamp. A user who unchecks and saves records lunch at noon.
2. **Silent "Other".** After free-text analysis, if the AI suggests no slot the review shows "Not chosen yet" and Save stays enabled. The meal becomes nutrition-only without telling the user. Observed in the browser: the Persian text "زرشک پلو با مرغ" came back with slot "Not chosen yet" although Lunch was the obvious slot. J3 promises "linked to its slot".
3. **Past-day card says "Your plan today".** On `/history/2026-09-18` the section heading is "Your plan for this day" while the score card eyebrow reads "YOUR PLAN TODAY" ([ScoreCard.tsx:42](src/components/product/ScoreCard.tsx#L42)). Seen in the browser.
4. **Needs review is resolved off-page.** Spec § 8: the row carries its one resolving action. Built: a link to meal details; "Confirm portion" exists nowhere.
5. **"Matches your plan" hides a real difference.** Observed: a lunch of rice + chicken (435 kcal) against option 1 "rice, chicken, salad, 650–720 kcal" shows "Matches your plan" on Today, while the meal detail page says "Chicken: 150 of 120 g planned, more than planned" and Why-this-score shows Portion 23 of 30. The label threshold is spec-compliant, but the user sees three different stories for one meal.
6. **Seven-day sentence less specific than spec.** "{slot} was a different food or skipped on N of M days" where the spec example separates the two.
7. **History row vocabulary.** "complete by default, 2 of 5 meals" is spec-internal wording and means nothing to a user.

### 1.3 Job 1: log a meal fast with the least cognitive load

Measured in the browser at 390×844:

| Path | Taps | Forced decisions | AI wait | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Planned meal from a slot row, multi-option | 3 (Log this meal → option → Save) | 1 (which option) | none | Items are pre-filled from the plan. Very good. |
| Planned meal, single option | 2 | 0 | none | Best case. |
| Planned meal from the floating button | 4 | 1 | none | +1 tap for the slot chip. |
| Free text | 3 + typing (+1 first time for the AI notice) | 0–n questions | 2–3 s real, stub instant | Review screen stacks items, questions, totals, slot, date/time, notes before Save. |
| Free text that should be a plan slot | 5 + typing | 2 (Change → slot → option) | same | Save is disabled until an option is picked even when the food was clearly different. The user must answer "which option were you supposed to eat" for a meal that was not on the plan. |

Friction observed:

- **Wrong thing first on the compose screen.** The user who tapped "Log this meal" on Breakfast lands on a sheet whose first three blocks are a text area, a photo button and a Recent-meals row; the option list they came for is the fourth block, just at the fold. For this audience the planned path is the high-frequency one.
- **Review screen is long.** For a three-item meal the Save button is roughly 1,700 px below the top of the sheet. Date, time and notes are always expanded, though the spec puts them under "More details".
- **"Analyze" is a system verb.** The spec's voice is calm and human; "Estimate my meal" or "Check this meal" would fit.
- **"Estimated" badge on every item says nothing.** It is the default state; only "Assumed" and label-based values carry information.
- **No acknowledgement of a recorded slot in the composer.** After Breakfast is logged, the Breakfast chip in "Today's planned meals" looks identical to unlogged slots.

Strengths worth keeping: no AI call on the planned path, idempotent save, draft restore after a reload, the abandon-to-manual path during a slow analysis, "Last time" suggested but never preselected.

### 1.4 Job 2: see how I am doing against the plan

The information is right and traceable. The presentation buries it:

- **Above the fold on Today** (390×844) the user sees: date, greeting, a time-zone hint banner, and a 9-line reflection paragraph. The score card starts at roughly 690 px, so the first paint answers "what does the app think of yesterday" rather than "how is today going". The reflection is the spec's morning moment, but it should not out-rank the score every time the app opens.
- **Empty state of the score card** says only "IN PROGRESS / Not enough information yet". Nothing tells the user that a number appears after two planned meals, so the first day feels unresponsive.
- **Why this score** is clear and honest (Food 50 of 50 · Portion 23 of 30 · Order or timing 20 of 20). Good.
- **History** answers the weekly question well enough for seven days, but the top summary is three unrelated sentences ("A few more complete days…", "1 day has an incomplete log.", "Your plan changed during these days.") with no visual anchor; the day rows give a bare number with no band word or colour.
- **Nutrition details** show recorded vs target with source labels, as specified; six nutrients at three lines each is heavy inside Today.

## 2. Senior UI Designer / visual review

Screenshots reviewed at 390×844 in light and dark: Today (collapsed and expanded), composer (compose, option list, review), meal details, History, History day, My plan, Settings, sign-up, onboarding steps 1–2.

### 2.1 Scorecard

| Area | Score /5 | Justification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Token compliance | 4.5 | No hex colours and no `dark:` overrides in product code; arbitrary values are only safe-area and `dvh`. Two `font-bold` uses against design.md's weight ceiling. |
| Dark mode | 4.5 | Full token parity, re-tuned semantic colours, `color-scheme` set. Skeletons are emerald-tinted, which reads as "success" on dark. |
| Bidirectional text | 5 | `NameLabel` isolates Persian originals with `<bdi>` and an explicit LTR English run; digits and punctuation never flip. Best part of the codebase. |
| Forms and touch | 4 | Inputs 44 px, `text-base` (no iOS zoom), correct `inputMode`, labels and errors wired. Secondary controls and the dismiss X are 36 px; focus ring is 1 px. |
| Feedback and motion | 3.5 | Route skeletons, slow-analysis fallback, toasts. Skip/unskip is not optimistic; the toaster is top-right on a phone and covers the sticky top bar. |
| Hierarchy | 3 | One card recipe for score, reflection, slot list, meals, nutrition, completeness and hints. Nothing tells the eye which block is "the number". |
| Iconography | 3 | Lucide icons at a consistent 16 px, all with `aria-hidden`, icon buttons labelled. But slot status, match results, meal rows, nutrition rows, history rows and plan slots are text-only. |
| Density | 2.5 | Today carries roughly 45 lines of text before the fold; My plan is a long text list; the reflection is a 9-line block at 16 px; each review item is 5 lines plus a badge row. |

### 2.2 What the screenshots show

- **Today, light, first viewport:** header, hint banner, reflection card filling 60 % of the screen, score card just visible at the bottom, floating "Log meal" button. Clean, calm, but the eye has nothing to land on: same border radius, same white, same 16 px text everywhere.
- **Today, expanded:** the page is 2,543 px tall for a day with two meals. Score card, Why-this-score, five slot cards, nutrition, recorded meals, completeness. It is legible, but it is a document, not a dashboard.
- **Two emerald buttons per viewport.** The in-row "Log this meal" and the floating "Log meal" are both filled primary and both visible on Today and on the History day view. design.md and decision 019 ask for one.
- **Status labels.** "Not recorded", "Matches your plan", "Some of this meal matches your plan · Added: گردو" are the same grey small text as the option line. Status is neither colour-coded (correct per the rule) nor glyph-coded (missing).
- **Composer option list.** The chosen slot chip turns mint and the option list appears below with a "Last time" pill. This is the best-designed moment in the app: clear, one decision, nothing preselected.
- **Review screen.** Item cards are tall: name, English, badge row, Quantity + Unit + Unknown row, nutrition line. The pencil and bin icons at 36 px sit tight to the card edge. Totals are a neat four-column strip.
- **History.** Rows are clean; the score number is bold and right-aligned with a chevron. The three-sentence summary card above has no icon and no hierarchy between its lines.
- **My plan.** Correct and complete, but 2,500 px of text at three type sizes with no time glyph, no kcal glyph and no visual separation between options beyond a hairline. It reads like the imported document, not like a plan.
- **Settings.** A well-ordered form page. The privacy block is three 20-word bullets in a card.
- **Dark mode.** Consistent and pleasant; the mint primary holds up.
- **Sign-up in dark after logging out of a dark-mode account.** The appearance cookie survives logout, so the next person on a shared device sees the previous user's theme until they sign in (`logoutAction` does not clear it).
- **Onboarding.** One question per screen, big heading, progress bar. The "Sex" screen is a heading and two radios on an otherwise empty 844 px canvas; fine functionally, austere visually.

### 2.3 Best-practice violations

| Heuristic | Where | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visibility of system status (Nielsen 1) | Slot rows | Recorded / not recorded / needs review distinguished by wording only, in muted grey. |
| Recognition over recall (Nielsen 6) | Composer chips | No mark on slots already logged today. |
| Aesthetic and minimalist design (Nielsen 8) | Today, My plan, review items | Repeated "Estimated" badges; six three-line nutrition rows; reflection paragraph always fully expanded. |
| One primary action per view (HIG / design.md) | Today, History day | Two filled emerald buttons on screen together. |
| Focus appearance (WCAG 2.4.13) | Kit base classes | `focus-visible:ring-1`; badges and tabs use 2 px, buttons and inputs do not. |
| Target size (WCAG 2.5.8 / spec § 12) | Time-zone hint dismiss, item edit and remove, Mark skipped | 36 px targets beside 44 px ones. |
| Consistency (Nielsen 4) | Page titles | Today and Meal details use `text-xl`; Plan and Settings `text-2xl`. |
| Feedback placement | Toaster | Top-right on a phone overlaps the sticky bar and the date header. |

### 2.4 Top visual improvements by impact

1. **Three surface treatments instead of one.** Score card as the only elevated or tinted surface with a large numeral; lists as flat hairline containers; reflection and hints borderless with a 2 px start rule. Extract a `Surface` variant so the recipe stops being copy-pasted.
2. **Glyph plus medium weight for every status.** Outline circle = not recorded, check = matched, half-dot = partly, slash = different, minus = skipped, question = needs review. Colour stays neutral, so the no-colour-only rule holds.
3. **One emerald per viewport.** In-row "Log this meal" becomes outline; the floating button stays filled.
4. **Reflection collapsed to one line by default** with `max-w-prose` when expanded.
5. **Nutrition rows as value / target two-column** with source and status behind a per-row disclosure.
6. **Chevrons and photo thumbnails on recorded-meal rows;** clock and flame glyphs on plan slot summaries; collapse plan slots after the first two.
7. **Kit hygiene:** 2 px focus ring with offset, neutral skeletons, 44 px icon buttons, one h1 size, weight ceiling written into decision 019.
8. **Toaster top-centre under the bar;** skip/unskip optimistic so feedback appears where the thumb is.

### 3.5 Defects found by reading the code (verified in source unless marked)

| ID | Severity | Where | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M1 | Medium | [interpret-plan.ts:45-89](src/services/ai/interpret-plan.ts#L45-L89) | A Persian range heading such as "شنبه تا پنجشنبه:" ("Saturday to Thursday") is treated as a Saturday heading, so a very common Sat–Thu / Friday plan layout becomes a weekday plan with slots only on Saturday and Friday. Reproduced by calling the function. Same for "Monday to Friday:". |
| M2 | Medium | [plan.service.ts:556-563](src/services/plan.service.ts#L556-L563) | Plan-draft writes are not conditional on `draftRevision`; the revision is checked in a separate read. Two edits with the same revision both succeed and the second silently overwrites the first. Meals use a conditional `updateMany`; drafts should too. Also affects `estimateDraftBaseline`. |
| M3 | Medium | [meal.service.ts:1097-1100](src/services/meal.service.ts#L1097-L1100) | Deleting a meal marks its uploads `REMOVED` but never deletes the S3 objects; nothing later does. The storage gauge excludes `REMOVED` rows, so the soft limit drifts from the real bucket size. |
| L1 | Low–Med | [plan-import.job.ts:303](src/services/jobs/plan-import.job.ts#L303) | Every retry attempt inside one import job spends one of the six daily import admissions; two flaky imports can lock the user out of "Try again" for the day. |
| L2 | Low | [timing.ts:23-24](src/lib/rubric/timing.ts#L23-L24) | Times are compared as minutes since midnight; a manually typed 00:30 against a 21:00 slot is "earlier by 1,230 minutes" and mis-orders the day. The late-night composer path avoids it by storing no time. |
| L3 | Low | [day.service.ts:36-84](src/services/day.service.ts#L36-L84) | Skips and completeness are accepted for future dates; meals refuse them. |
| L4 | Low | [uploads/route.ts:63-79](src/app/api/uploads/route.ts#L63-L79) | The 10 MB pre-check trusts `Content-Length`; a chunked body is fully buffered before the size check. Authenticated and rate-limited, so low. |
| L5 | Low | `requireAuth()` in route handlers | An expired session on `/api/uploads`, `/api/photos`, `/api/export` redirects to HTML instead of returning 401, so the composer shows a generic network error. |
| L6 | Low (speculative UI impact) | [day-view.service.ts:281-291](src/services/day-view.service.ts#L281-L291) | After a time-zone change, today's row keeps the old zone and flips to "past" at the old midnight while the user is mid-day. |
| L7 | Low | [meal.service.ts:977-982](src/services/meal.service.ts#L977-L982) | Saving a draft older than 24 h silently drops photos whose staged rows expired. |
| U1 | Low | `logoutAction` | The `appearance` cookie is not cleared on logout; the next person on the device inherits the theme. Observed in the browser. |
| U2 | Low | [ScoreCard.tsx:42](src/components/product/ScoreCard.tsx#L42) | Past-day score card eyebrow reads "Your plan today". Observed. |

### 3.6 Test coverage gaps worth closing

- Photo logging end to end (button, MEAL_PHOTO call, photos on meal details, Remove photo, flag off hides the button, export with photos). No e2e at all.
- Weekday plans anywhere in the UI (import review "apply the same checks", My plan tabs, Today on a weekday). M1 would have been caught here.
- Meal details "Change plan link" and "Reuse as new meal" (no unit or e2e).
- J6 backdate from a History day, including "I don't remember the time" and the future-time refusal in the UI.
- J10 Replace over an active plan with the "N meals affected" line (only the Edit path is e2e'd).
- Settings preferences (units, time zone, week start) and the Today "device time zone changed → Update" line: no tests at all.
- Composer states: manual entry, Retry analysis, 15 s / 45 s waits, offline label, "newer edit on another device → Reload".
- Rule review choices "Keep as a note" / "Don't compare" and weekly rule progress on My plan.
